## Full description: English

Draw a line. Watch the balls roll. Change their colors on the way and fill every circle.

Color Curves is a calm, clever physics puzzle game. Each level gives you a launcher, some
colored pieces and a goal. You draw the path, so every solution is yours.

**100 hand-made levels in five chapters**
- Learn the basics: rails, paint and color.
- Meet new ideas one at a time: color doors, switch walls, prisms that split one ball into
  two, bumpers, booster pads, gravity zones, zero gravity and sideways gravity.
- Then put it all together in Chapter 3's hard mixed puzzles.
- Cross the Volcano: lava, fans, conveyor belts, glass and timed doors.
- Reach Space: wormholes, black holes, magnets and color mixing.

**Made to feel good**
- Smooth, satisfying physics. Every level is tested to make sure it can be solved.
- Limited ink and three hearts per level: plan before you launch.
- Collect the hidden star in every level.
- Stuck? Get a word hint, see the first line, or see the whole solution.
- Calm original music and gentle sounds.

**Made for everyone**
- English and Arabic, with full right-to-left support.
- Color symbols on every ball and piece, so it's playable with color blindness.
- Reduced-motion option, separate music and effects volume.
- No account needed, and it plays offline. Your progress stays on your device.

A game by Rawan Studio.
